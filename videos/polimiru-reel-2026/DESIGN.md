## Style Prompt

Polimiru reel visual identity: restrained civic-tech editorial design for a Japanese political information service. Use a white and deep navy foundation with glassy transparency accents, fine rule lines, precise data cards, and sober motion. The mood is trustworthy, clear, and modern, avoiding campaign-like energy. Composition should feel like information becoming legible: source documents, search, comparison, and area-based election context.

## Colors

- Deep navy: `#0f1f3d` for primary text, final brand fields, and serious information surfaces.
- Civic blue: `#1e3a6e` for secondary panels, strokes, and UI emphasis.
- Clear blue: `#2f6fde` for active search, progress, and “visible” highlights.
- Paper white: `#ffffff` for main canvas and document cards.
- Mist blue: `#e8edf6` for soft backgrounds and translucent glass layers.
- Muted slate: `#4a5a72` for explanatory text.

## Typography

- Japanese UI: `"Hiragino Kaku Gothic ProN"`, `"Hiragino Sans"`, `"BIZ UDGothic"`, `"Meiryo"`, sans-serif.
- Logo / Latin accent: `"Raleway"`, `"Avenir Next"`, sans-serif.
- Tone: compact, editorial, and high contrast. Large text only for one clear idea per scene.

## What NOT to Do

- Do not use politician campaign colors or party-like red/yellow palettes.
- Do not make it feel like a generic SaaS ad with oversized gradient blobs.
- Do not use the previous Polimiru reel as a storyboard, line, timing, or visual reference.
- Do not imply Polimiru ranks politicians by popularity or endorses anyone.
- Do not overload frames with long explanations; keep every scene readable in a fast vertical feed.
